## Description — English

Make every screen your cinema.

Reelskin restyles YouTube, Twitch and your favourite streaming sites with beautiful themes — and adds the player tools
you always wanted.

🎨 24 THEMES, ONE CLICK
• Dark and light themes with aurora glows, gradients or your own picture behind the page
• Frosted top bar and menus, rounded thumbnails, accent-coloured progress bar and logo
• Optional fonts with full Vietnamese support: Be Vietnam Pro, Lexend, Nunito
• Deep recolouring: Reelskin reads the site's own colour tokens, so every corner follows your theme

🖌️ THEME MAKER
• Six colours, live preview and a readability check
• Start from one colour, "Surprise me", or a picture — Reelskin extracts its palette and uses it as the backdrop
• Export and share themes as files

🎬 PLAYER TOOLS (any video)
• Ambient light: the video's colours glow softly around it
• Cinema mode: dim everything but the video
• Speed control from 0.1× to 16×, start speed, remember per site
• Picture filters: brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth… with 8 presets (Vivid, Cinema, Night…)
• Save the current frame, loop, picture in picture
• Keyboard shortcuts (S/D/R for speed, Alt+C cinema, Alt+S capture…) — all remappable

🧘 FOCUS ON YOUTUBE
• Hide Shorts, the home feed, related videos, comments, end screens, chips or merch
• Choose videos per row, blur or grey out thumbnails until hover, fade watched videos

🌗 AUTO SWITCH
• Light theme by day, dark at night — by time or with your system

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no server. Everything stays on your computer.

Works on: YouTube, Twitch (full themes) · YouTube Music, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Vimeo, FPT Play, VieON
(background, accent, fonts and every player tool) · any other site with a video (player tools, optional).

Reelskin is an independent project and is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Twitch, Netflix, Amazon, Disney,
Vimeo, FPT Play or VieON.

## Privacy practices (dashboard answers)

- Single purpose: "Customise the look of video sites and add player tools (themes, ambient light, cinema mode,
  speed, filters)."
- Host permission justification: "Reelskin applies the user's theme and player tools on the listed video sites. It
  only reads style sheets and the video element; no page content is collected."
- `storage`: "Stores the user's settings and themes locally."
- `scripting`: "Registers the player-tools script on all sites only after the user grants the optional all-sites
  permission from the extension's UI."
- Remote code: No. Data collection: none (tick nothing). Certify the three disclosures.
- Privacy policy URL: the public URL of `docs/PRIVACY.md` (needs a public repository or page).
